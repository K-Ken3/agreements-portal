# Privacy and legal readiness

The Terms and Privacy pages are draft placeholders and are not legal approval. Before production, obtain qualified Rwanda legal/privacy review and verify current applicable requirements with the relevant authorities.

This MVP stores account legal name, email, password hash, agreement draft content, and agreement activity in PostgreSQL. It does not collect phone numbers, national identity numbers, biometric data, or signatures; it does not send notifications or verify identity. The public website makes no claim that an agreement or signature is legally enforceable.

Before expanding data collection or production use:

- Document each data purpose, lawful basis, necessity, disclosure and retention period.
- Establish access, export, correction, deletion, legal hold, and incident-response procedures.
- Complete privacy impact review for identity or other high-risk processing.
- Confirm processor agreements, hosting region, backup location, subprocessors, and cross-border transfer requirements.
- Publish reviewed terms, privacy, cookie, and electronic-consent disclosures.
- Keep any government identity or qualified-signature provider disabled until official status, lawful route, credentials, contracts, and end-to-end verification are confirmed.
- Never store biometric templates or raw fingerprint data.
